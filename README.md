### 👋 About me

I'm a full-stack developer who likes owning a product from the database to the phone in your hand.
My day-to-day is **C# / .NET** on the backend, **Angular** on the web and **Flutter** on mobile, with a soft spot for clean architecture and well-tested code.

- 🔭 Currently building **[Zorio](https://github.com/ShibamSasmal/Zorio)**: a connected network of local print shops (web, mobile and desktop apps on one .NET backend)
- 🌱 Exploring cloud deployment, payments and real-time integrations
- 💬 Ask me about ASP.NET Core, Entity Framework, Angular or Flutter
- ⚡ I enjoy turning messy business workflows into simple apps
